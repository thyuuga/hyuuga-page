Thy Recorder

Screen recording, made simple.

Thy Recorder is a lightweight macOS screen recording application designed for capturing windows, system audio, and microphone input with a clean, straightforward interface.

  • macOS
  • Apple Silicon & Intel
  • Free + PRO

03 / Free + PRO

Start simple. Make room for more.

Free

  • Standard recording quality
  • MP4 output
  • 30 fps
  • Thy Recorder watermark
  • Up to 1 hour per recording

PRO

  • Original recording quality
  • MOV output
  • Up to 60 fps (subject to recording mode)
  • Watermark removal and customization
  • No Free recording duration limit

PRO licensing and purchasing are being prepared for the standalone release.
